Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my first product block?

Navigate to "Create New Block" in the sidebar, select your retailer, search for products, and add them to your block. Then customize the layout and CTA text.

How do I connect my affiliate accounts?

Go to Settings > Affiliate API Keys and enter your API keys for Amazon, Walmart, Shopify, or Skimlinks. Your keys are securely stored and used to fetch product data.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the Billing page. Changes take effect immediately.

How do I embed blocks on my website?

Copy the embed script from Settings > API & Embed and paste it into your website's HTML. The block will automatically load and display your products.
