No coding required

KotaPal empowers creators, bloggers, and e-commerce sellers to merge products with retail affiliate offers into AI-powered product blocks. Seamlessly integrate them across your blog, online store, YouTube channel, and email campaigns.

Core Features

Everything you need to monetize your content effectively

Shopify + Affiliate Integration

Combine owned Shopify products with Amazon and Walmart affiliate offers in unified blocks.

Amazon + Walmart Product Sync

Keep listings fresh with automatic product, price, and link updates from both networks.

AI Copy & Recommendations

Let AI write blurbs, pros/cons, and suggest higher‑converting alternatives.

Universal Embed System

Embed anywhere: WordPress, Shopify, Ghost, Webflow, Substack, email, YouTube overlays.

Analytics Dashboard

See clicks, CTR, conversions, and get smart suggestions to optimize performance.

Smart Automation

Automate price tracking, stock alerts, and performance optimization across all platforms.
